# Northfield Municipal Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees must complete a 90-day probationary period before requesting remote work. Department directors may waive this requirement for roles hired specifically as remote positions.

## Equipment
The city provides a laptop, external monitor, and a one-time $150 home-office stipend for approved remote employees. Employees are responsible for their own internet connection, which must provide at least 25 Mbps download speed.

## Schedule
Remote employees must be available during core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday. Outside of core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long as they complete 40 hours per week.

## Data Security
Remote employees must use the city-issued VPN for any access to internal systems. Storing city data on personal devices or personal cloud accounts is prohibited under Policy Section 7.2.

## Performance Review
Remote employees are evaluated on the same quarterly review cycle as in-office staff. Managers are required to hold at least one video check-in per week with each direct report working remotely.

## Termination of Remote Status
The city may revoke remote work eligibility with 14 days' written notice if performance falls below expectations for two consecutive quarters, or immediately in the case of a confirmed data security violation.

## Reimbursement
Approved business travel from a remote employee's home office follows the same mileage reimbursement rate as in-office staff: $0.67 per mile as of the most recent policy update. Internet stipends are not provided on an ongoing basis beyond the initial $150 setup payment.

## Union Employees
For positions covered by a collective bargaining agreement, remote work arrangements must additionally comply with the relevant CBA and may require notice to the union prior to implementation.
